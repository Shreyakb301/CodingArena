Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

CodingArena is a coding-interview practice app. This policy explains what it stores about you and why. It is deliberately short because the app collects very little.

What we store

  • Your account. If you create an account with email and password, we store your display name, email address, and a one-way hash of your password (we never store the password itself). If you use Continue with Google, we store your Google account ID, email address, and name, so we can recognise you on your next visit.
  • Your progress. Your ratings, streaks, quiz history and roadmap progress are saved so they are there when you come back and stay in sync across your devices.

How it is used

Your data is used only to run the app for you: to sign you in and to show your own progress. We do not sell it, share it, or use it for advertising. There are no third-party analytics or tracking scripts on the site.

Where it lives

The app and its data are hosted on Cloudflare. Your progress is also cached in your own browser so the app works offline. Google receives a sign-in request only if you choose Continue with Google, and only to confirm your identity.

Deleting your data

Email sreyakb301@gmail.com and we will delete your account and all associated progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
