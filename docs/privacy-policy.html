CodingArena Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

CodingArena ("we", "our", "the app") is a coding-interview practice app. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it. CodingArena is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children.

Information we collect

  • Account information: an email address (optional) and a password, if you create an account. Passwords are stored as salted cryptographic hashes — we never store or can recover your plaintext password.
  • Practice activity: the code you write and submit for practice problems, along with results (pass/fail, timing) used to power spaced-repetition scheduling and progress tracking.
  • Device/technical data: standard request metadata (IP address, timestamps) generated by using the app's API, retained only as needed for security and abuse prevention.

What we don't collect

We do not collect precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or payment details. We do not run third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

How we use your information

  • To operate your account and sync your practice progress across devices.
  • To execute and grade the code you submit, using an automated code execution service (Judge0) solely to run your submission and return the result to you.
  • To improve the app's learning engine (e.g. which problems to resurface and when).

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your code, email, or progress with third parties, except the code-execution service strictly necessary to run the code you submit, and infrastructure providers (hosting, database) who process data on our behalf under standard confidentiality terms.

Data retention and deletion

We retain account and practice data for as long as your account is active. To request export or deletion of your data, contact us at the email below — we will act on verified requests within a reasonable time.

Security

Passwords are hashed and salted before storage. Access to the API requires authentication (JWT). We do not store your submitted code anywhere outside the systems needed to grade and display it back to you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
